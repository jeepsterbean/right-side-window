## Design Context

### Users
Singapore founders, operators, and marketing leads who need a full-stack marketing function without hiring an in-house team. They arrive to book a call, not to browse a long manifesto.

### Brand Personality
Direct, confident, slightly playful. Three words: sharp, human, commercial. The interface should feel like a grown-up agency site that still has a pulse — not a SaaS template, not a neon startup.

### Aesthetic Direction
Reference [nowadays.ai](https://www.nowadays.ai/) very closely for the hero: airy near-white canvas, Satoshi, centered display type, indigo primary CTA, quiet secondary button, staggered word-reveal, and a dense client-logo field. Brand color lives in the RIGHT SIDE mark (gold, red, cyan, green, purple). Anti-references: dark neon SaaS, Inter-on-gray, gradient headline text.

### Design Principles
1. Match nowadays.ai’s spatial rhythm and type scale before inventing.
2. Let the logo carry color; keep the page quiet.
3. One job on this page: get the call booked.
4. Motion is entrance-only, ease-out-expo, reduced-motion safe.
5. Placeholder logos are clearly fake — never impersonate real brands.
