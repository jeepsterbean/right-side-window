A few practical questions.

How many revisions do I get?

Graphic design is unlimited within the month. The monthly video is edited until that piece is approved. A new concept that needs another shoot is a separate conversation.

Is ad spend included?

No. The fee covers planning and management. Media budget is yours, spent only if you want paid reach.

Who do I talk to day to day?

Your dedicated brand strategist. WhatsApp is the working channel — +65 8779 9950 — so you are not chasing six people for an update.

How long am I locked in?

Month to month. Start, pause, or stop without an annual contract.

What if we need more than one shoot?

The retainer includes one film session a month. Extra shoot days, extra locations, or a second concept are scoped and billed separately.

How do you measure whether it’s working?

Against the goal we set on the call — usually enquiries, bookings, or sales, not vanity reach. You get reporting on what’s working and where to put the next dollar.
